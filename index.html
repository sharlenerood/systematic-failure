The Law

Close Corporations Act

Section 9 of the Close Corporations Act requires the court registrar to transmit winding-up orders to CIPC “without delay”.

Section 341(2) of the 1973 Act (preserved by Schedule 5, Item 9 of the 2008 Act):
“Every disposition of its property by any company being wound-up and unable to pay its debts made after the commencement of the winding-up, shall be void unless the Court otherwise orders.”

Section 394 of the Companies Act (also preserved):
The liquidator is required to open a separate estate bank account. Once winding-up begins, the company's property falls under the control of the liquidator and the directors cease to have authority over the company's assets.
